Common questions

Can I book a table?

Yes. Choose "Table booking" in the form above with the date and number of guests, or send us a WhatsApp. Groups of 8 or more, please book a day ahead.

Do you cater for offices and events?

We bring a barista, a machine and everything else for 20 to 200 guests. Choose "Catering & events" and tell us the date, place and number of people. We reply with a quote within one working day.

Do you have dairy-free or decaf options?

Every drink can be made with oat or almond milk, and any espresso drink can be decaf. Ask about our sugar-free syrups too.

How far do you deliver?

Up to 5 km from the café, for $1.50. Orders usually arrive in 20 to 30 minutes.

Do you sell beans and gift cards?

Yes. Whole beans or ground for your machine, in 250 g and 1 kg bags, and gift cards from $10. Ask at the counter or on WhatsApp.
